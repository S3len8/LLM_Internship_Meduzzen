# 12. Modern Practical Applications of Quantum Physics

Although quantum physics may seem abstract, it underlies most modern technologies.

Main areas of application:
1. **Semiconductors and transistors**: solid-state theory made it possible to create microprocessors.
2. **Lasers**: based on the phenomenon of stimulated emission of light.
3. **Medical diagnostics (MRI)**: uses nuclear magnetic resonance.
4. **Atomic clocks**: provide highly accurate timekeeping for satellite navigation systems such as GPS.
5. **Quantum sensors**: enable highly precise measurements of magnetic and gravitational fields.
