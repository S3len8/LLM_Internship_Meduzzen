# 2. Wave-Particle Duality and the de Broglie Hypothesis

Wave-particle duality is the fundamental property of matter to exhibit both wave-like and particle-like properties.

In 1905, Albert Einstein explained the photoelectric effect by proposing that light consists of particles called photons. On the other hand, diffraction and interference experiments demonstrated the wave nature of light.

In 1924, Louis de Broglie hypothesized that every moving matter particle, such as an electron, has wave properties. The de Broglie wavelength is defined as $\lambda = \frac{h}{p}$, where $p$ is the particle's momentum. Davisson-Germer electron-diffraction experiments confirmed this hypothesis.
