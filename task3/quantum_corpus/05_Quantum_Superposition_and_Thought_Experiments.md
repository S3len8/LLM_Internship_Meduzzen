# 5. Quantum Superposition and Thought Experiments

The principle of quantum superposition states that if a quantum system can exist in states $\psi_1$ and $\psi_2$, it can also exist in their linear combination $\psi = c_1\psi_1 + c_2\psi_2$.

This means that a particle can exist in several states at the same time until a measurement is made. The measurement process causes the wave function to collapse into one of the possible eigenstates.

The famous thought experiment known as Schrödinger's cat demonstrates the paradoxical application of quantum superposition principles to macroscopic objects.
