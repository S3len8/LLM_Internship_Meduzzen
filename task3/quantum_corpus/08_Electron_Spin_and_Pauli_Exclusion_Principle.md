# 8. Electron Spin and the Pauli Exclusion Principle

Spin is the intrinsic angular momentum of a quantum particle and is not associated with its motion through space.

Based on spin, all particles are divided into two groups: fermions, with half-integer spin $1/2, 3/2, \dots$, and bosons, with integer spin $0, 1, 2, \dots$. Electrons, protons, and neutrons are fermions.

The Pauli exclusion principle states that two identical fermions cannot occupy the same quantum state at the same time. The Pauli principle explains the structure of atomic electron shells and the stability of matter.
