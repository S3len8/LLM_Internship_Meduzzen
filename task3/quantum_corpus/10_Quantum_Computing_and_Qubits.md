# 10. Quantum Computing and Qubits

Quantum computing studies the use of quantum-mechanical phenomena to process information.

The basic unit of quantum information is the qubit. Unlike a classical bit, which has a value of either 0 or 1, a qubit can exist in a superposition state: $|\psi\rangle = \alpha|0\rangle + \beta|1\rangle$.

Thanks to quantum superposition and entanglement, quantum computers can perform a huge number of computations in parallel. Shor's algorithm for factoring and Grover's algorithm for searching demonstrate a significant advantage over classical algorithms.
