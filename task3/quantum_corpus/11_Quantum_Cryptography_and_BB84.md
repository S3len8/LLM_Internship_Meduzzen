# 11. Quantum Cryptography and the BB84 Protocol

Quantum cryptography uses the principles of quantum mechanics to ensure secure data transmission.

The best-known application is quantum key distribution (QKD). The first such protocol was BB84, developed by Charles Bennett and Gilles Brassard in 1984.

The security of quantum cryptography is based on the no-cloning theorem, which states that a quantum state cannot be copied perfectly, and on the measurement principle: any attempt by an attacker to intercept or observe the system changes its state and is immediately detected by legitimate users.
